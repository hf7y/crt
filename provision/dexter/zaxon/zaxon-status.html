Published by crt/provision/dexter/zaxon/zaxon-watch.sh, which runs on dexter, where the relay is. zaxon is the one sensor nothing else can report on: every other alarm in the estate is delivered through it, so when it goes quiet the tracker does not fill with alarms — it goes silent, which reads from outside like a calm night. The verdict is deliberately never OK for a relay that merely answers: an open port is not a reachable human. The queue holds one question at a time (crt#67, so three agents are not three pings), which means an ignored question occupies the channel for its full TTL before going stale — stale_slot_hours is how much of the day that cost every other caller.
