**Built and ran a real acoustic loopback self-test**, per Zach's
explicit ask ("check the earcon approach... mic-earphone loopback").
New `bin/crt-earcon-loopback-test.py`: plays a pure tone on a given

**Real, fairly strong finding from that tool**: the TV/HDMI path
(`plughw:2,0`, a separate physical device) showed a real signal above
baseline (5.0x); the handset path (`plughw:1,0`, the SAME USB device as
the actively-running mic capture) showed almost nothing (0.1x) —
backwards from what physical proximity would predict. Strong evidence
this specific USB audio adapter can't reliably play+record
simultaneously (plain ALSA `hw:`/`plughw:` devices are exclusive-access
by default; this is very likely resolvable, not necessarily a true
hardware full-duplex limit). Root-caused: this is why the earlier "yes,
heard both in order" confirmation worked — that happened while capture
was accidentally dead (the separate `plughw:0,0` bug, fixed later that
same session), so there was no device contention at the time.

## Investigated, real findings, no code change (deliberately)

**Vosk on potato's actual hardware is NOT the slam-dunk the earlier
research note assumed.** Discovered potato is a **Raspberry Pi 3 Model
B+** (not 4/5-class) — quad-core Cortex-A53 @1.4GHz, 905MB RAM, only
183MB free with active swap at the time of testing. Installed `vosk`
(venv `~/.venvs/crt-vosk`, real aarch64/cp313 wheel) and its small
English model, tested live on real hardware: first partial result for a
1.5s clip didn't land until t+1.53s, final result at 2.65s wall-clock —
not dramatically faster than realtime on this specific box, contrary to
the "<100ms" framing carried over from the earlier research. Streaming
partial results are still Vosk's real structural advantage over
whisper's batch-VAD approach (that reasoning holds), but this needs
re-testing once potato isn't under memory pressure before any design
commits to it. Test scripts left on potato at
`~/vosk-models/vosk_test.py`/`vosk_test_short.py` for reuse. Whether
potato's own hardware (a Pi 3B+) is the right long-term choice at all is
flagged as an open question below, not something to guess at unattended.

**MAXUTT/TRAIL reviewed, not changed.** `CRT_VAD_TRAIL` (0.8s) looks
fine as-is. `CRT_VAD_MAX` (20s hard cap) is the one worth a human
ear-tuning session — confirmed live yesterday that continuous speech
with no real pause rides the full cap before an utterance even gets cut.
Not changed here: this is a "does it feel right" tuning call, not a
correctness fix, same posture this project applies to every other
default-behavior change.
